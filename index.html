Stay low under lights. Jump red pipes and gaps. The track never stops — miss a jump and you fall.

  1. Pick a ticket. Coins on the track are that ticket's LIM. Catch them to keep them. The run ends if you hit a red pipe, a searchlight, or a gap.
  2. New players get two free SCOUT runs. Join Telegram or post to X for +1 extra each, once per address. Free SCOUT appears on the daily board with score 0 and does not share the prize pool.
  3. Catch every coin and the ticket LIM stays yours — nothing burned this run. Then keep running: new score ×2 until you crash. It gets faster.
  4. Each friend who starts a run from your link adds +5% LIM on final settlement. Four friends and every coin caught settles at 120% of the ticket. Cap 200%.
  5. Missed coins go to the pools: daily 50% · invite 20% · burn 30%.
  6. The daily board gets 50% of missed LIM. Of that, 40% is split evenly among everyone who played that day; 60% follows the day's scores. Claim yesterday at 00:00 Singapore.
  7. The invite board gets 20% of missed LIM, split evenly among the top 200 inviters.
