🚀 Quick path

Connect wallet → 2 free SCOUT runs → copy invite link → pick RUNNER or higher → claim at Singapore midnight → repeat.

🎮 One run

  1. ① Clear the ticket

    Coins on the track ≈ this ticket's LIM. Catch them all — this run burns nothing, and what you catch settles to your wallet.

  2. ② Overtime

    Keep running after the ticket is in. Ticket LIM returns to your wallet. Paid overtime splits 50% floor / 50% burn — you do not sign a second transfer. SCOUT (1 LIM) caps at ×2 (keep 1; 0.5 floor / 0.5 burn). Other tickets cap at ×1.5 (VAULT 15 → keep 10; 2.5 floor / 2.5 burn).

  3. ③ Settle

    The run settles on-chain. Ticket LIM lands in your wallet. Paid overtime is split by the house: 50% today's floor, 50% burn. Confirm settle in your wallet. Check the tx on BSCScan.

📈 Settle bonus

Caught LIM × 105%+. Invites and extra runs are separate, one reward % cap at 200%. Ticket LIM returns to your wallet. Paid overtime splits 50% floor / 50% burn. Caps: 1 LIM ×2, others ×1.5 (VAULT 15 → keep 10).

💰 Missed LIM

Leftover from each run is split by the contract:

  • 🏆 50% → daily board pool
  • 👥 20% → invite board pool
  • 🔥 30% → burn (dead address, on-chain)

📅 Singapore midnight opens one hour on the POOLS tab. Claim daily board + invite with CLAIM / CLAIM INVITE; claim the floor pool separately with CLAIM FLOOR.

📊 Floor pool

Separate from the 50% daily score pool. Half of paid overtime is sent here; the other half is burned. One equal share per address that finishes a settled run today. Claim at Singapore midnight with CLAIM FLOOR. Unclaimed leftover rolls into the next day's floor pool.

❓ FAQ

Out of free runs?
Buy SCOUT (1 LIM) or any other ticket with your LIM.
Where does overtime LIM go?
Ticket LIM returns to your wallet. Paid overtime does not — 50% goes to today's equal-split floor, 50% is burned. Boosts still count for ranking. Settle today, then claim your floor share at Singapore midnight.
Why does my wallet pop up after a run?
That's settle after each run. Confirm ticket LIM back to your wallet. Paid overtime is split 50% floor / 50% burn by the house — no extra signature from you.
Does 200% mean 200% LIM in my wallet?
200% is the ranking rate cap. Ticket LIM returns to your wallet. Paid overtime (VAULT 15 → keep 10) splits 50% floor / 50% burn. Play today and claim your floor share at midnight.
What is the floor pool?
Equal-split bonus pool — not the 50% score board. Half of paid overtime is sent here; the other half is burned. Settle a run today to earn a share. Claim at Singapore midnight with CLAIM FLOOR. If you miss that window, your share stays in the pool for the next day's players.
Why can't I claim?
Usually outside the claim window (Singapore midnight hour), or nothing pending yet.
Where does LIM come from / how do I sell?
Catch it in-game — it goes to your wallet. Swap in-app when you need BNB or USDT.
Is it fair?
Tickets, settle, pools, and burn all run on BSC contracts — public rules, verifiable on-chain.
