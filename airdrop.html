Eligibility

This airdrop is for participants who submitted a screenshot and wallet address in the Telegram and X campaigns. After verification, the list contains just over 3,000 addresses. Only listed addresses can claim.

How to claim

Look up your address and amount on this page, then open Catbox and connect the same wallet. LIM is credited on-chain to that address, with no intermediate account.

Deadline

Claims must be completed by 15 September 2026, 23:59 Singapore time (UTC+8). Unclaimed amounts after that time will be redistributed as set out below.

After the deadline

Unclaimed LIM is split 50% / 50%: 50% is airdropped again to addresses that did not claim; 50% is allocated to Catbox prize pools for the daily board, invite board, and other mini-games.

Allocation

The total is 100,000 LIM. Daily ranks 1–10 receive 100 LIM each; ranks 11–40 receive 50 LIM each. Remaining listed addresses split the rest equally, about 26.4874 LIM each.
